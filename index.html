01 // MANIFESTO

The Meaning Behind BETWEEN

The glitches represent the moments when reality feels distorted. The darkness represents uncertainty. The light represents the part of us that hasn’t given up.

Every piece is a different reality, but they all belong to the same human experience.

DARKNESS / UNCERTAINTY WHY BETWEEN? LIGHT / PERSISTENCE

Why BETWEEN?

Because life rarely happens at the extremes. We exist somewhere between falling apart and finding ourselves, between losing hope and finding a reason to try again.

“We are never just the darkness or the light.
We are everything in between.”
